TLDR

A quiet 72 hours dominated by two hard signals on September 1: MongoDB posted 30% YoY growth with Atlas beating guidance and raised FY27 outlook, reinforcing the consolidated-database threat, while Qdrant dropped a 10-billion-vector open benchmark dataset plus an open-source harness (Supernova) that ships Elasticsearch support out of the box — an explicit escalation of its benchmark war with Elastic. Weaviate, Algolia and Vespa were silent — though Vespa.ai Live (Sept 10), OpenSearchCon NA (Sept 22–24) and Algolia DevCon (Sept 30) all land within the next month.

Vendor details
Pinecone Active

On September 1 Pinecone introduced two billing changes: it began metering egress (data returned on reads) with monthly allowances by plan — 1 GB Starter, 10 GB Builder, 100 GB Standard and Enterprise, with overage billed on Standard/Enterprise and reads blocked on Starter/Builder — and it withdrew the one-time $250 bulk-import credit for new Standard/Enterprise subscriptions.

Just outside the window (Aug 25–26): BYOC reached GA across AWS, GCP and Azure, and CEO Ash Ashutosh published a "One Year In" company post.

Pinecone is monetizing a new axis rather than shipping features — a TCO-comparison opening for Elastic in deals where high-recall, high-top-K retrieval returns large result payloads, and a signal that serverless unit economics are tightening.

Weaviate Quiet

No updates in the past 72 hours.

Most recent items sit outside the window: Weaviate 1.39.0 (Aug 4), 1.38 with HFresh disk index and MCP server GA (Jun 25), and Engram memory service GA (Jun 3).

OpenSearch Active

On August 31 AWS expanded Amazon OpenSearch Service Cluster Insights with 17 new automated root-cause insights for Red and Yellow cluster status — JVM OOM, CPU saturation, zone imbalance, misconfigured replicas — free of charge, across 11 regions, and covering Elasticsearch 6.8+ domains as well.

No core OpenSearch project release landed in the window; 3.8 shipped in early August and the 3.9 window opens September 15.

AWS is closing the managed-operations gap that has been one of Elastic Cloud's clearest differentiators — and the fact that these insights also cover legacy Elasticsearch 6.8+ domains supports AWS's migration-capture motion.

Algolia Quiet

No updates in the past 72 hours.

Nearest items outside the window: Agent Studio governance and cost-control enhancements (Jul 28), and Algolia DevCon 2026, a free virtual event on Sept 30 – Oct 1.

Qdrant Active

On September 1 Qdrant Labs, with Vultr, released Qdrant-FineWeb-10B — claimed as the largest open-source vector search benchmark at 10.07B dense plus 10.07B sparse vectors, ~24.5 TB, with exact top-1000 ground truth for 100k+ queries — plus two further datasets (PubMed-MV, Coyo-VE) and Supernova, an open-source GPU-native benchmarking framework.

Supernova's nova-load and nova-storm modules explicitly support Elasticsearch and Milvus as evaluation backends.

Qdrant is trying to own the referee's chair in vector-search benchmarking — having already publicly attacked Elastic's DiskBBQ benchmark in July, it now ships tooling that lets anyone run Elasticsearch at 10B scale on Qdrant's terms. Worth evaluating the harness before the numbers circulate.

MongoDB Active

MongoDB reported Q2 FY2027 results on September 1: total revenue $771.8M, up 30% year over year — its highest growth in several years — with Atlas revenue up ~29% and roughly 300 basis points ahead of guidance. Non-GAAP operating income was $185.9M, a 24% margin versus 15% a year ago. Full-year FY2027 guidance was raised to ~$2.99–3.03B, driven mainly by Atlas.

MongoDB reaccelerating to 30% growth with expanding margins strengthens the "one database for operational data plus search plus vectors" consolidation pitch, which is the main displacement threat to Elastic in net-new AI application workloads.

Databricks Active

On September 1 Databricks shipped Genie One web search (Beta) — Genie One can now query the public web and cite external sources — and enabled Genie One and Genie Agents to run on OpenAI models served through Databricks. Apache Arrow support for Zerobus Ingest also reached GA.

Larger recent moves sit just outside the window: a $5B round at a $190B valuation (Aug 13) and completion of the Panther acquisition (Aug 3).

Databricks is extending its agent layer from lakehouse data out to the open web, pushing further into the "answer engine over enterprise plus external context" territory Elastic is targeting — and the Panther build-out is a direct flank on Elastic Security's SIEM base.

Nearest items outside the window: the Vespa.ai / Voyage AI by MongoDB partnership for in-pipeline query embedding (Aug 20) and a blog post on Aug 10. Vespa.ai Live is scheduled for Sept 10 in London.
